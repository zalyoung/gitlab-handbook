Software Engineers in Test in the Developer Experience stage are responsible for GitLab's core testing infrastructure - the systems and tools that process over thousands of tests that run daily across GitLab's product surface. This includes building test execution frameworks, infrastructure provisioning systems, monitoring solutions, and developer tooling that enable efficient development practices.

In this role, you'll focus on creating reliable, scalable testing infrastructure that powers GitLab's development. Your work directly impacts thousands of developers through faster test feedback cycles, stable test environments, and intuitive testing tools. You'll build systems that help engineering teams implement effective testing practices while optimizing infrastructure costs and resource utilization.

Successful candidates combine hands-on development experience with a passion for building developer tools and testing infrastructure. 

## Responsibilities

- Design and develop tools, frameworks, and platforms that enable developers to implement and maintain their own test automation.
- Create reusable components and libraries that make it easier for development teams to write reliable tests.
- Build and maintain infrastructure that supports efficient test execution and reporting across GitLab's frontend, backend APIs and services.
- Develop platform capabilities that support testing of low-level systems like geo-replication, CI/CD, and load balancing.
- Create and maintain tools for automated environment provisioning using technologies like Terraform and Ansible.
- Design scalable test infrastructure that can be easily adopted by development teams.
- Build monitoring and observability solutions for test environments and pipelines.
- The work you'll do will be [transparent and open to the GitLab community](https://gitlab.com/groups/gitlab-org/-/issues?scope=all&utf8=%E2%9C%93&state=opened&label_name[]=Quality)
- Develop and maintain tools for automated pipeline triage and failure analysis.
- Create developer tooling that encourages testing at all levels (Unit, Integration, and End-to-end).
- Collaborate with development teams to understand their testing needs and build solutions that address those needs.
- Build tools and processes that help inject testing earlier into the software development process.
- Identify, share, and create tooling to support best practices in code health, testing, testability, and maintainability.
- Demonstrate GitLab's CREDIT values through transparent technical documentation, iterative development practices, efficient resource usage, collaboration across teams, and measurable results

## Requirements

- You have a few years of professional experience in active development in platform/infrastrcture centric teams. Experience building developer tools and frameworks is necessary.
- You bring significant experience using Git and its command line
- You bring strong experience with Git and modern version control workflows
- You bring experience with test automation frameworks and tooling.
- You bring strong foundation in containerization and orchestration technologies. Experience with Docker is preferred, but equivalent experience with other containerization technologies is valuable
- You bring experience with or strong interest in:
  - Infrastructure as Code (using tools like Terraform)
  - Configuration management (using tools like Ansible, Chef, or Puppet)
  - Container orchestration (using Kubernetes or similar platforms)
  - Cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, or Azure)
- You have experience with Continuous Integration systems (e.g., GitLab, Jenkins, Travis etc)
- Our [values](/handbook/values/) of collaboration, results, efficiency, diversity, iteration, and transparency resonate with you
- You'll thrive in an environment where [self-learning and self-service](/handbook/company/culture/all-remote/self-service/#self-service-and-self-learning-in-onboarding) is encouraged and instilled as a part of our culture
- Ability to use GitLab

## Hiring Process

Candidates for this position can expect the hiring process to follow the order below.
Please keep in mind that candidates can be declined from the position at any stage of the process.
As a result an interview can be canceled at any time even if the interviews are very close (e.g. a few hours apart).

To learn more about someone who may be conducting the interview, find their job title on our [team page](/handbook/company/team/).

- Candidates will complete a technical assessment when they apply. Sometimes this will be shared by our Talent Acquisiton team after an application is made.
- Qualified candidates will be invited to schedule a 30 minute screening call with our Talent Acquisiton team.
- Next, candidates will be invited to schedule a 45 minute first interview with the Hiring Manager for the team you are interviewing for. Once the interview is completed, there may be occasions where the interviewer believes you are a better match for another team we are hiring for. In this instance, we will ask you to meet another Hiring Manager as part of the process.
- Next, candidates will then be invited to schedule a 1 hour peer interview with a Software Engineer in Test in the Developer Experience department.
- Next, candidates will be invited to schedule a 90-minute technical interview with a Senior Software Engineer in Test. This will be booked for a date at least 5 business days into the future. This allows enough time to complete the asynchronous technical task, including all review cycles.
- Finally, candidates will be invited to schedule a 45 minute interview with the Director of Developer Experience.
- Successful candidates will subsequently be made an offer via email.

Additional details about our process can be found on our [hiring page](/handbook/hiring/interviewing/).
