## Team Member Resource Groups and Advocacy Groups

## Overview

Team Member Resource Groups (TMRGs) and Team Member Advocacy Groups (TMAGs) are essential components of GitLab's DIB (Diversity, Inclusion & Belonging) framework. These groups foster community, provide resources, and support our team members from diverse backgrounds while contributing to GitLab's inclusive culture.

## What are TMRGs and TMAGs?

**Team Member Resource Groups (TMRGs)** provide support for underrepresented groups at GitLab. These groups develop and elevate underrepresented team members, provide a voice and safe space for people who identify as part of that group and their allies, and aim to increase belonging.

**Team Member Advocacy Groups (TMAGs)** focus on inclusion and belonging for demographics or social issues that sit outside of URGs (Underrepresented Groups) and do not focus on team member representation.

## Connection to DNA Strategy

Our TMRGs and TMAGs play a critical role in GitLab's [DNA (Disability, Neurodivergence & Accessibility) Strategy](/handbook/company/culture/inclusion/dna-strategy/). They help drive the "R" (Representation) component of our ARISE framework by:

- Building diverse talent pipelines
- Creating advancement opportunities 
- Supporting underrepresented team members
- Providing valuable insights on inclusion efforts
- Connecting with external communities

## Starting a New TMRG or TMAG

If you're interested in starting a new TMRG or TMAG at GitLab, please:

1. Review our [TMRG and TMAG Guide](/handbook/company/culture/inclusion/tmrg-tmag/tmrg-and-tmag-guide/)
2. Submit a proposal using the [TMRG Request Issue Template](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/diversity-and-inclusion/-/blob/master/.gitlab/issue_templates/TMRG-request-template.md)
3. Contact the DIB team via Slack at #diversity_inclusion_and_belonging with any questions

## Get Involved

We encourage all GitLab team members to participate in our TMRGs and TMAGs, whether as members of the represented groups or as allies. These groups provide valuable opportunities for professional development, community building, and contributing to GitLab's inclusive culture.
