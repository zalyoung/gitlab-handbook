# Diversity, Inclusion, and Belonging Events & Partnerships Criteria

#### Five Essential Requirements from Potential Partners
* **Community & Reach**
    * Total active community size
    * Geographic distribution of members
    * Demographic breakdown of membership

* **Program Infrastructure**
    * Dedicated partnership contact
    * Event management capabilities
    * Communication/promotion channels

* **Track Record**
    * Past corporate partnership examples
    * Recent event participation numbers
    * Success stories/case studies

* **Resource Requirements**
    * Partnership cost structure
    * Expected time commitments
    * Support needs from GitLab

* **Growth & Measurement**
    * Current metrics tracking system
    * Reporting capabilities
    * Growth plans for next 12 months

#### Events & Partnerships Rating Criteria 

**Rating System**

**Each criterion is rated on a scale of 1-3:**

1 = Limited potential/alignment
2 = Moderate potential/alignment
3 = Strong potential/alignment

Minimum Score Required: 10/15 to pursue

**Five Key Criteria**

* Strategic Alignment
    * Aligns with DIB strategy and goals
    * Matches target demographics/communities
    * Supports current business objectives
    * Example of 3/3: Partnership directly addresses multiple URG communities in key markets

* Impact & Reach
    * Size of accessible community
    * Geographic coverage
    * Industry influence
    * Example of 3/3: Global reach with 10,000+ active community members

* Resource Investment vs ROI
    * Financial commitment required
    * Team time/effort needed
    * Expected returns (recruiting, brand, etc.)
    * Example of 3/3: Low financial investment with high potential returns

* Implementation Feasibility
    * Existing infrastructure
    * Clear points of contact
    * Defined success metrics
    * Example of 3/3: Partner has established programs and dedicated team

* Sustainability & Growth
    * Long-term partnership potential
    * Scalability opportunities
    * Mutual growth alignment
    * Example of 3/3: Clear pathway for multi-year engagement and expansion
