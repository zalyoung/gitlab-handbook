### My Work Style

I tend to need to be involved in the process of determining the place an integration lives in and how it needs to function. I will always try to respect working async but I will have to meet with the people requesting to understand their needs for the integration and have them walk me through the process. I have to do this to get in the mindset of the individuals requesting the integration. This is so I can better understand the expectations put on them regarding the input and output of this process as well as eliminate any potential of miscommunication. This also allows me to get a better picture of what could be needed on either end of the integration, IE is there something that could be possibly automated before this integration that would enhance this integration, identifying potential missed requirements or enhancements, or alternatively is there information that would help people that are relying on the output. Identifying these opportunities allows for better solutions and outcomes as well as allowing for more holistic view and understanding of an environment and the processes surrounding it. 

Going through this process can require multiple meetings. Documentation is always nice but sometimes I require meetings to better understand the problem and get my head in the role of the people I am working with (especially since my work tends to come from teams that do things outside of skillset, I am not an accountant, or a sales person or a AR or AP individual). My skills rely on living in someone else's shoes for just a brief period of time.
