## Introduction

On this page, we explain GitLab's approach to compensation structure and how it aligns with our values and Compensation Principles.

## Our Transition to Market-Based Compensation

We are transitioning from our historical Compensation Calculator to a market-based compensation approach. This page details our current approach and the transition process.

### Historical Note: The Compensation Calculator

The Compensation Calculator was retired on 2025-05-30. Previously, GitLab used a Compensation Calculator that was based on San Francisco benchmarks with location factors applied. Team members had access to this calculator to determine compensation based on role, level, and location.

This calculator used a formula: SF benchmark × Location Factor × Level Factor × Exchange Rate to calculate compensation.

## Current Compensation Approach: Market-Based Compensation

As of 2025-05-30, GitLab has implemented a market-based compensation approach that aligns with industry standards and best practices.

### Understanding Market-Based Compensation

Market-based compensation ensures that our ranges reflect the value of a role in the current job market in each team member's country and location. Rather than basing compensation solely on a location factor applied to San Francisco market ranges, we now regularly benchmark our roles against market data to understand what similar jobs pay at companies comparable to GitLab.

This approach helps us:
- Attract and retain top talent
- Maintain fairness and transparency
- Practice fiscal responsibility
- Ensure competitive compensation aligned with local markets
- Remove dependency on exchange rate fluctuations

### Key Changes

- **Retirement of the Compensation Calculator**: The Compensation Calculator was retired on 2025-05-30.
- **Visibility in Workday**: Team members now have visibility to their current Total Target Cash range in Workday.
- **Market-Aligned Ranges**: By 2025-08-30, all market-aligned compensation ranges will be implemented and visible to team members in Workday for their current role.
- **Commission-Eligible Roles**: Ranges for commission-eligible roles will be displayed in Workday no later than 2025-08-30.

### Alignment with GitLab Values

Our market-based compensation approach continues to align with our values:

1. **Transparency** - Compensation ranges are visible to team members in Workday, maintaining our commitment to transparency while ensuring data security.
1. **Efficient** - The market-based system streamlines compensation decisions, reducing the need for multiple approval levels and conversations.
1. **Diversity, Inclusion & Belonging** - People with the same attributes should earn the same. Our market-based approach reduces unconscious bias and supports fairness across all teams, levels, and countries.
1. **Collaboration** - Team members can provide feedback on market-based compensation through established channels.
1. **Iteration** - Our compensation approach will continue to be reviewed and refined during the Annual Compensation Review.
1. **Results** - Our compensation approach helps us focus on outputs while ensuring team members are fairly compensated according to market rates.

## Access to Compensation Information

### Viewing Your Compensation Range

Team members can view their total target cash range in Workday. This functionality became available on 2025-05-30. For team members with a bonus as part of their compensation, the total target cash range includes base salary plus bonus.

For team members in commission-eligible sales roles, compensation range information will be visible in Workday no later than 2025-08-30.

### Requesting Information About Compensation Ranges

Team members can request information about compensation ranges for:
- Their next level
- Open requisitions
- Career development planning

Please contact HelpLab for a consultation regarding current compensation ranges specific to your career development.

## Reporting a Discrepancy

As with all things at GitLab, our compensation approach is a constant work in progress. There are a few options for reporting a discrepancy if you find your compensation doesn't align with market expectations.

**If you are an internal GitLab team member or external to GitLab:**

- Create an issue utilizing the appropriate issue templates
- Email total-rewards@domain for more personal inquiries

**If you prefer to remain anonymous:**

- You can use the [Reporting a Compensation Discrepancy Form](https://forms.gle/tEmiwzYxCJqZhivF8).
  - Providing an email is optional, but we will only reach out directly with any updates if an email is provided.

## Contact Total Rewards

For any questions relating to compensation, reach out to [Total Rewards](/handbook/people-group/#how-to-reach-the-right-member-of-the-people-group).
