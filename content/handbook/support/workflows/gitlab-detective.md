## Overview

This document provides information on what GitLab Detective is and includes guidance on how the project is managed as well as guidance on how to handle customer inquiries about GitLab Detective. 

## What is GitLab Detective?

GitLab Detective is a Support-maintained tool that customers can use to assess whether their GitLab environment passes a collection of checks.  

### What problems are we trying to solve with GitLab Detective?

One of the core philosophies of GitLab Detective is "no re-debugging". Each GitLab Detective check provides customers and Support Engineers with the precise information needed to effectively confirm whether a particular environment is experiencing or likely to experience a particular problem. 

GitLab Detective is intended to be helpful for folks assessing and reducing the impact of upgrading GitLab. See how GitLab Detective is mentioned in the [A guide to the breaking changes in GitLab 18.0](https://about.gitlab.com/blog/2025/04/18/a-guide-to-the-breaking-changes-in-gitlab-18-0/) blog post.

### What is the current state of GitLab Detective?

As of June 2025, GitLab Detective is [an experiment](https://docs.gitlab.com/policy/development_stages_support/#experiment) that is being actively developed.

## Handling tickets

Since GitLab Detective is [an experiment](https://docs.gitlab.com/policy/development_stages_support/#experiment), customers should not use Support tickets if they need assistance with GitLab Detective. Folks interested in GitLab Detective are encouraged to see the [✨ Getting Started with GitLab Detective: Info + Feedback!](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/support/toolbox/gitlab-detective/-/issues/96) issue and to open a **New** issue in [the issue tracker](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/support/toolbox/gitlab-detective/-/issues/?sort=created_date&state=opened&search=started&first_page_size=100) if they have questions or require assistance.

## Demos

We are partnering with select customers to demo GitLab Detective functionality and solicit feedback to help influence the direction of GitLab Detective. Reach out to Brie Carranza in [#spt_gitlab-detective](https://gitlab.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C07G68YEDNG) to discuss conducting a demo with a customer you work with. 

## History

- The tool was renamed from "GitLab Doctor" to "GitLab Detective".
