These articles cover the lifecycle of creating, reviewing, and publishing an article.

**Knowledge Article CREATE Process**
- Draft Article
- Saved Article IN PROGRESS
- Article Awaiting Review  (Article will be reviewed by the KCS Team to make sure formatting and details are accurate. An Editorial review. Once the article is reviewed, it is moved into Awaiting REview and a Knowledge Champion/Technical Reviewer will be assigned, or take the article and PUBLISH)
- Article is PUBLISHED

**Knowledge Article MODIFY or UPDATE Process**
- Article needs to be in EDIT status. (To do this, go to the manage article list, open the published article. The article will then be editable.)
- Make your edits. The Article will be back in the "In Progress" status
- Save the Article
- The Article will be reviewed by the KCS Team for formatting, spelling, etc. and a Knowledge Champion (Technical Reviewer) will be assigned.
- The Article will be PUBLISHED.

Note:  The **Ready to Publish** Status will only be used for the articles that are ready to publish on a defined Date. This should be used for product release dates, changes that need to be released on a certain date.

**Knowledge Article PUBLISH process**
Articles must be Technically reviewed before it is published live.  A Technical Reviewer will follow the process defined of going into the Manage Articles List, choosing the article, reviewing, and then Publishing.
- Choose the article from the In Progress List
- Review the Article. If everything is accurate, Use the Down Arrow next to the Save button (bottom Right corner) and choose PUBLISH.
- If the article needs re-work, please assign back to the author by using the assign icon (feature next to the Title ) and include notes.
