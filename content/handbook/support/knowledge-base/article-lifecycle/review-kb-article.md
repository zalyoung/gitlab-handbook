### Reviewing a Knowledge article*

- Articles need to be Reviewed for Technical Accuracy before they are consumed by Customers (or Support).
- Technical Reviewers are assigned across the Product Features and Geos. 
    1. Technical Reviewers will:
        - Go to the Manage Articles section within ZenDesk
        - Locate articles “assigned to you” and/or  check the “in Progress” section.
        - Approve the article - and Publish.
