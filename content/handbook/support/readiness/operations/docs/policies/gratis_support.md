<sup>*Introduced via [support-team-meta#2408](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/support/support-team-meta/-/issues/2408)*</sup>

This process is completely automated by the
[Support Super form processor](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-support-readiness/processors/support-super-form-processor)
project. Submissions on the
[Support Super Form](https://support-super-form-gitlab-com-support-support-op-651f22e90ce6d7.gitlab.io/)
result in a ticket being created for the customer/prospect.

If you are looking for a more in-depth description, please review the
[Support handbook page on requesting gratis support](/handbook/support/gratis-support/).
