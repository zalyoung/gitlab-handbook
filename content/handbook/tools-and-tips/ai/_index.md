# SAFE AI Use

## Before Using AI Tools: Input Checklist
Before submitting content to AI tools, verify your input:
* Contains no sensitive or non-public information
* Does not include confidential team member data (performance, start dates, departures)
* Contains no customer or partner information (logos, trademarks, spend)
* Free of material nonpublic information (financial results, customer wins/losses, executive changes)
* Does not contain information about security/stability of GitLab's platform
* Avoids mentioning significant transactions or legal proceedings

## Before Sharing AI Outputs: Review Checklist
Before using or sharing AI-generated content:
* Contains no sensitive or non-public information
* Information is accurate and verifiable
* No financial information is included without approval
* You've considered both intended and unintended effects
* Content aligns with GitLab's transparent values and SAFE framework

## Best Practices For Using AI With SAFE
1. **Always review AI outputs** before sharing them - AI may inadvertently include speculative or inaccurate information
2. **Verify information** from AI against authoritative GitLab sources
3. **Do not share sensitive information** with AI tools, as they may incorporate this into future responses
4. **Be especially cautious** with financial information, customer data, and non-public strategic plans

## What To Do If SAFE Information Is Shared
If you believe sensitive, inaccurate, financial, or potentially harmful information has been shared:
1. For social media posts: Take a screenshot, remove the post, and contact Senior Director of Legal, Corporate via #safe Slack channel
2. For issues and MRs: Mark as "Confidential" and contact Senior Director of Legal, Corporate via xyz
3. For GitLab Unfiltered videos: Mark as private and contact Senior Director of Legal, Corporate via xyz
4. For other mediums: Send a link to the original post via xyz
