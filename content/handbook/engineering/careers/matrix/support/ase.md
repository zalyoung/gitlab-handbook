## Assigned Support Engineer (ASE)

 {{% include "includes/engineering-careers/support-career-nav.md" %}}

**Assigned Support Engineers at GitLab are expected to exhibit the following competencies:**

- [Leadership Competencies](#leadership-competencies)
- [Technical Competencies](#technical-competencies)
- [Values Alignment](#values-alignment)

---

### Leadership Competencies

<!-- {{% include "includes/engineering-careers/senior-leadership-competency.md" %}} -->
- Able to communicate clearly and effectively, both orally and in writing, in
  all business settings
- Able to deliver positive outcomes even when initially given unclear
  requirements
- Demonstrates knowledge of how Support and the Enhanced Support Offerings fit
  into the larger business, and acts in alignment with that knowledge
- Able to look beyond problem statements to understand their customers' actual
  needs and to use that understanding to develop multiple options for solutions
- Able to identify process improvement opportunities within their team and to
  work with others to implement process changes
- Able to assist others with solving difficult support tickets
- Able to assemble all needed people from all parts of the company to resolve
  customer issues
- Able to mentor and guide teammates to be more successful in their role
- Able to disseminate their technical and nontechnical knowledge to individuals
  and the team in a variety of ways, such as pairings, learning modules, help
  sessions, and knowledge articles
