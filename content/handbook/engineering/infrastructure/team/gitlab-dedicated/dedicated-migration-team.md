## Process

### Team compositon for migrations
Each customer migration will be assigned DRIs 2-3 weeks ahead of deal closure. The technical program managers are responsible for assigned the DRIs.

For Geo migrations the following DRIs will be assigned
- Project Manager
- Technical Architect
- Engineer
- Geo Engineer

For Congregate migration the following DRIs will be assigned
- Project Manager
- Technical Architect
- Engineer

Technical program managers will oversee all ongoing migrations.

### Initial scoping
The technical architect,chitect together with the SA/CSM will perform the initial scoping to identify the provisioning information for the customer's Dedicated tenant instance. The initial scope will start 2 - 3 prior to deal close.

#### PTO Coverage Issue

We have a separate project specific to the team for tracking PTO coverage issues. Team members are expected to open a coverage issue if they are taking more than 3 days or more PTO. Why 3 days or more? Because we want to ensure continuity for the customer and progress on the project.

To create a coverage issue follow this link to the [pto coverage template](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/gl-infra/gitlab-dedicated/dedicated-migrations-group/pto-coverage/-/issues/new?description_template=pto_coverage.).
Once the issue is completed the coverage issue, post a note to the [#dedicated-migrations-global](https://gitlab.enterprise.slack.com/archives/C08NKJQRMQW) Slack channel to inform other team members of the up coming PTO.

The coverage issue will help ensure we have someone to cover while you are out so you can enjoy your time off.
