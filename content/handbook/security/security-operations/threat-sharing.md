## Threat and Intelligence Sharing Overview

The Signals Engineering and Threat Intelligence teams sponsor bi-directional threat and intelligence information sharing with GitLab customers. This bi-directional sharing facilitates the rapid sharing of time sensitive and customer relevant threat updates, customer facing detection engineering content, and threat intelligence flash reports.

## How to Get Involved

GitLab customers can contact their sales or customer success representative and request access. 

## Threat and Intelligence Sharing: Accceptable Use Policy

### Authorized Data Sharing 

GitLab uses [Traffic Light Protocol](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/news/traffic-light-protocol-tlp-definitions-and-usage) (TLP) to define sharing restrictions. Generally, data classified up to TLP:Amber (information restricted to GitLab and our customers) can be shared. 

### Data Sharing Rules

All shared data should include the proper TLP classification, which enables involved organizations to understand how they may share the data within their own organization and beyond. `NOTE: Any data shared without a specified classification level will default to TLP:Amber.`

TLP:AMBER Data

1. Access limited to trusted team members within GitLab or involved customers. 
2. Must not be shared on public forums or social media
3. Must be stored in secure systems with access controls
4. Must be marked clearly with "TLP:AMBER" classification

TLP:GREEN Information

1. May be shared within the customer's sector or community
2. Must not be published on public websites
3. Should be marked with "TLP:GREEN" classification
4. May be shared with partners who support the organization's cybersecurity efforts

TLP:WHITE Information

1. May be distributed without restriction
2. Should be marked with "TLP:WHITE" classification
3. Can be posted on public websites and social media

### Prohibited Data Sharing

While GitLab may disclose in a shared channel or forum threat intelligence information that may be pertinent to third-parties or customers, the following classifcations of data should not be shared in these channels and forums:

- [Red Data](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/security/data-classification-standard/#red)
- [unSAFE Data](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/legal/safe-framework/#what-if-unsafe-information-is-shared)
- Data that may otherwise be deemed confidential to a Customer under our terms

If you have a question about whether threat intelligence information marked for sharing fits into one of these prohibited categories, please reach out to the Legal Team for assistance.

### GitLab Responsibilities

1. Maintain confidentiality of shared thret and intelligence according to its TLP classification
1. Lead threat and intelligence sharing efforts
2. Properly classify GitLab shared information according to TLP protocols
3. Provide secure channels for threat and intelligence sharing
4. Monitor compliance with this policy
5. Moderate intelligence sharing
6. Provide guidance on proper handling of shared intelligence

### GitLab Customer Responsibilities

1. Maintain confidentiality of shared intelligence according to its TLP classification
2. Implement required security controls for handling classified information
3. Report any unauthorized disclosures
5. Use shared intelligence only for intended cybersecurity purposes
6. Provide feedback on actionable intelligence when requested
2. Properly classify Customer shared information according to TLP protocols

### Shared Data Authorized Use

1. Threat detection and prevention
2. Security control implementation
3. Incident response and investigation
4. Risk assessment and management
5. Security awareness training
6. Vulnerability management

### Shared Data Prohibited Use

1. Used for commercial purposes without explicit authorization
2. Shared beyond the scope of its TLP classification
3. Modified or altered in ways that change its original meaning
4. Used to target or retaliate against other organizations
5. Published or disclosed to unauthorized parties
6. Used for marketing or public relations purposes
