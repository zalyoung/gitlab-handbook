## What We Do

Composed of [Security Architecture](/handbook/security/product-security/security-architecture/), [Security Research](/handbook/security/product-security/security-research/), and [Product Security Engineering](/handbook/security/product-security/product-security-engineering/), we focus on addressing systemic product security risks.

- We own and operate the Product Security Risk Register to continuously identify, assess, and prioritize risks, enabling us to plan cross-organizational risk reduction efforts that enhance GitLab's platform security posture and protect our customers, and prioritize security work.
- We assess the GitLab ecosystem from a security perspective to identify previously unknown risks and vulnerabilities.
- We develop the security strategy and architect solutions for addressing critical risk areas. 
- We contribute directly to the product's evolution by:
  - Building product-first capabilities that mitigate risk and meet the needs of both GitLab security teams and customers.
  - Designing secure architecture solutions and executing proofs-of-concept that accelerate engineering teams' delivery of security improvements or strategic roadmap initiatives.
  - Influencing GitLab's security and compliance roadmap, recognizing we are a canary for external enterprise-grade customer needs.
  - Monitoring the Product Security Department's ability to effectively use GitLab product features to secure the GitLab ecosystem. We coordinate across Product Security teams and serve as Customer 0 for new security features to validate their functionality and utility for security teams. Where there are gaps, we provide clear and documented feedback to reduce friction and unlock customer value.
- Finally, we lend our expertise to our peer teams across the organization to enable them to accomplish their security goals in the form of automation, informal training, documentation, and mentorship.
