## Team organization and planning

Our team plan and organize our work on our [appsec-team tracker](https://gitlab.com/gitlab-com/gl-security/product-security/appsec/appsec-team). We use this tracker for:

  * Planning our milestone work
  * Track any type of work we are doing
  * Brainstorm, collaborate and discuss any AppSec related topic
