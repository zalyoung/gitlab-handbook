title: "System and Communication Protection Policy"
---

Policy and Procedures
1.1. The organization develops, documents, and disseminates system and communication protection policies and procedures. (SC-1)
1.2. System and communication protection policies and procedures are reviewed and updated annually or when significant changes occur. (SC-1)


Boundary Protection
2.1. The information system monitors and controls communications at the external boundary and at key internal boundaries. (SC-7)
2.2. The organization implements subnetworks for publicly accessible system components that are physically or logically separated from internal networks. (SC-7(3))


Encryption
3.1. The information system implements cryptographic mechanisms to prevent unauthorized disclosure of information during transmission. (SC-8)
3.2. Cryptographic keys are managed in accordance with organization-defined requirements for key generation, distribution, storage, access, and destruction. (SC-12)


Network Segmentation
4.1. The organization implements security functions as a layered structure minimizing interactions between layers of the design. (SC-3)
4.2. The information system separates user functionality from information system management functionality. (SC-2)


Denial of Service Protection
5.1. The information system protects against or limits the effects of denial of service attacks. (SC-5)
5.2. The organization employs monitoring tools to detect indicators of denial of service attacks. (SC-5(3))


Session Management
6.1. The information system terminates network connections associated with communications sessions at the end of the sessions or after a defined period of inactivity. (SC-10)
6.2. User sessions for high-risk applications are terminated after a period of inactivity. (SC-12(2))


Information in Transit
7.1. The information system protects the confidentiality and integrity of transmitted information. (SC-8)
7.2. The organization implements cryptographic mechanisms to prevent unauthorized disclosure of information during transmission unless otherwise protected by alternative physical safeguards. (SC-8(1))


Mobile Code
8.1. The organization defines acceptable and unacceptable mobile code and mobile code technologies. (SC-18)
8.2. The organization authorizes, monitors, and controls the use of mobile code within the information system. (SC-18)


Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP)
9.1. The organization establishes usage restrictions and implementation guidance for Voice over Internet Protocol (VoIP) technologies. (SC-19)
9.2. The organization authorizes, monitors, and controls the use of VoIP within the information system. (SC-19)


Secure Name/Address Resolution
10.1. The information system implements mechanisms for authentication and integrity protection of name/address resolution queries and responses. (SC-20, SC-21)
10.2. The information system provides additional data origin authentication and integrity verification artifacts along with the authoritative name resolution data the system returns in response to external name/address resolution queries. (SC-20)


Architecture and Provisioning for Name/Address Resolution Service
11.1. The information systems that collectively provide name/address resolution service are fault-tolerant and implement internal/external role separation. (SC-22)


Session Authenticity
12.1. The information system protects the authenticity of communications sessions. (SC-23)
12.2. The information system invalidates session identifiers upon user logout or other session termination. (SC-23(3))


Fail in Known State
13.1. The information system fails to a known-safe state in the event of a system failure. (SC-24)


Protection of Information at Rest
14.1. The information system protects the confidentiality and integrity of information at rest. (SC-28)
14.2. The organization implements cryptographic mechanisms to prevent unauthorized disclosure and modification of information at rest unless otherwise protected by alternative physical measures. (SC-28(1))"""
