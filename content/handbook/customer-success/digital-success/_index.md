## Digital Success team

The GitLab Digital Success team accelerates value realization for customers at scale through a digital-first strategy. We enable customers to achieve faster adoption, higher satisfaction, and scalable growth by providing self-service educational resources, proactive engagement, and data-driven experiences.

### Mission and vision

Our mission is to establish GitLab as a leader in digital customer success by advancing a data-driven, customer-centric approach that enhances product adoption and customer retention across all segments. We aim to reduce churn, enhance growth, and improve retention rates by leveraging digital tools and experiences.

We harness digital tools beyond traditional educational experiences and email marketing to augment the capabilities of our Customer Success team, enabling them to focus on highly specialized customer interactions that require a human touch.

By integrating a centralized digital ecosystem, we seek to elevate customer engagement and success, driving overall business growth and sustainability without diminishing the value of personal connections.

## Core tenets of our strategy

1. **Digital-first**: Designing customer experiences based on digital channels and interactions
   - Self-service onboarding & education first
   - Automated health monitoring & alerts
   - Proactive, data-driven engagement

2. **Outcome-centric approach**: We prioritise measurable customer outcomes to drive retention and satisfaction. This includes tracking product usage trends, customer feedback, and business impact to refine our approach.

## Our initiatives

### Customer technical enablement

[GitLab University](https://university.gitlab.com/) is our digital learning platform that empowers users to develop their GitLab skills through:

- **Self-directed learning**: Flexible educational resources for all skill levels
- **Hands-on practice**: Safe environments to apply and reinforce skills
- **Role-based learning**: Tailored content to meet the needs of different user roles
- **Skills validation**: Methods to verify and showcase expertise

We continuously evolve our curriculum to address emerging needs and reflect best practices in DevSecOps.

### Live interactive events

We facilitate knowledge sharing and problem-solving through scheduled live events that:

- Provide immediate value for specific challenges
- Showcase product capabilities and use cases
- Foster community among users with similar interests

These events span multiple regions to accommodate our global customer base and are designed to support users at various stages in their GitLab journey.

### Certification

Our [certification program](https://university.gitlab.com/pages/certifications) establishes standards of expertise across key GitLab competency areas. These programs:

- Validate user skills and knowledge
- Create common terminology and standards
- Support professional development and recognition
- Enhance team productivity and effectiveness

We regularly update our certification content to align with product evolution and industry standards.

### Customer communications

We deliver timely, relevant information to customers throughout their lifecycle with GitLab. Our communications:

- Guide users through product changes and updates
- Provide advance notice of significant changes
- Offer clear instructions during critical situations
- Connect users with appropriate resources

All communications are designed to build trust and support customer success.

### Adoption strategy

We develop frameworks and guidance to help customers implement GitLab capabilities effectively. Our adoption resources:

- Address common implementation challenges
- Provide best practices based on real-world usage patterns
- Support compliance and governance requirements
- Accelerate time-to-value

These resources are tailored to different customer segments and use cases to maximize relevance and impact.
