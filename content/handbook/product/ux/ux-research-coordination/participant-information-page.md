## I’ve received an email from research@rally.gitlab.com - who is this, and now what? 

Welcome to our participant information page! If you’ve received an email from **research@rally.gitlab.com**, it means that our User Experience (UX) and Product team would love to hear from you, either in a survey, unmoderated test, or user interview.  

While we *send* from **research@rally.gitlab.com**, our *reply-to* email address is **research@gitlab.com**. We understand this may be confusing, or may bring you to the conclusion that it is a scam email. We have to send from a different email address as has been advised by our security team, and is the typical industry standard. Please note, in very specific/rare use cases, you may be invited to complete a screener or survey directly from one of our UX Researchers' email. If you unsure about a research email from GitLab, use our checklist below to assess if it's genuine, we've also included some call-outs that our emails typically contain. 

**How to assess whether it's a genuine email from GitLab Research:**

- Check the sender: We send from research@rally.gitlab.com. This is GitLab's official recruitment email for research studies.
- Check the reply-to: The reply-to is usually research@gitlab.com. This is an email inbox monitored by our Research Operations team.
- Still in doubt?: Forward the email to research@gitlab.com and we can confirm if it's genuine.

**Our emails will also have:**
- The GitLab logo
- A specific call to action (completing a survey, or registering interest for an interview)
- An opt-out/unsubscribe option where relevant
- Where possible, the name of the GitLab team member (and their GitLab profile) of who is conducting the research. Sometimes our researchers will instead opt to share their LinkedIn page. 
    - Official GitLab team members have a badge on their profile stating such.

We use [RallyUXR](https://www.rallyuxr.com/) as our CRM for all things research so that we can keep our information up to date and create a smooth experience for our participants. Our surveys/screeners are hosted in RallyUXR and our more complex ones are hosted in Qualtrics via RallyUXR. There will be a callout in your email stating which vendor is hosting the specific project we’ve reached out to you about, and these screener/survey links will often open in a new tab when clicked.

## I filled in a screener and I’ve been selected to take part in a User Interview. What’s next?

Amazing! We are looking forward to having you take part with us. Once you book your time through our researchers’ Google calendar or Calendly link (which you’ll receive directly via email if selected to participate), you’ll receive an automatic reminder before your session. If for any reason you can no longer attend or need to reschedule, the calendar invite will have instructions on how to adjust or cancel your session time. 

During your interview, our researchers will introduce themselves, and request permission to record the session.* These recordings are for research purposes only, and not shared with anyone outside of GitLab. Depending on the topic of conversation, our researchers may be looking for:
- Specific/general feedback on certain areas of the product.
- Walk you through prototype designs.
- Or simply want to learn more about yourself, your job, and your organization.

_*In some cases, participants may decide they do not want to be recorded when filling out the screener. In these cases, our researcher may bring along an additional note-taker to help capture feedback._ 

### Incentive Information

Once your interview has **completed**, you’ll receive a thank you gift from us (in the form of a gift card) from  research@rally.gitlab.com. This email will include information about how to claim your gift card from Tremendous. Tremendous offers an array of [reward options](https://www.tremendous.com/catalog/) based on your location. These gift cards are typically processed 1-2 business days after your session has concluded. 

If you **can’t accept** a gift card but are still interested in taking part in the session you’ve been invited to - just let us know during the interview, so that we know not to process it. 

## My session was cancelled by GitLab, will I still receive a Gift Card?

We do our best to never cancel sessions, but sometimes an unforseen event arises, and our researcher has no other option. Where possible we will always try to reschedule first. Below is a table of incentives processed based on notice provided by the researcher. If you, as a participant cancel your session, we will not process an incentive. 

| Notice Period Given | % of Incentive To Pay Out |
| ------ | ------ |
| <= 24 hours ahead of the session | 100% |
| <= 36 hours ahead of the session | 50% |
| > 36 hours ahead of the session | 0% |

More information on incentives can be found [here](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/product/ux/ux-research-coordination/participation-gratuities/).
