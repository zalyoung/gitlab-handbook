### Customer Success Strategy & Analytics

## Vision

Our vision is to be the trusted operational backbone that powers GitLab's Customer Success engine, delivering trusted actionable insights and informed, thoughtful recommendations that accelerate value realization for both internal teams and Customers. Our focus is on delivering measurable value through innovative yet practical solutions, ensuring alignment with Customer needs and organizational goals. We operate with accountability, prioritizing transparency and results in everything we do.

## What We Do

The CSSA team is repsonsible for supporting the business via a number of different methods and modalities. While our stakeholders span across numerous functions within the business our core stakeholders are within the Customer Success team which includes CSAs, CSMs, CSEs, Digital CS, RMs and their respective leadership teams. Our core functions include but are not limited to:

- Dashboards
- Reports
- Scorecards
- QBR support
- Ad-hoc analysis
- Project and planning support
- GTM Strategy
- Territory and Resource planning
- Product and Use Case scoring and analytics
- Customer Journey analytics
